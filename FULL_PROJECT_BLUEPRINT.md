# COW Farm OS — Full Project Blueprint

**Planning baseline:** Bardiya, Nepal  
**Blueprint date:** 2026-08-15  
**Repository role:** single source of truth

## Executive decision

Build a **capital-disciplined, feed-secure, heat-managed dairy farm**, learning from Banjara Agro's integrated systems without copying its oversized early CAPEX.

### Phase-1 target
- 30 breeding females
- mixed adapted HF-cross/Jersey-cross foundation cohort
- site/civil utilities reserved for 50 adults
- ~415 L/day saleable steady-state model
- 8–10 bigha irrigated fodder target
- 4 FTE workers + manager/owner
- initial funding envelope ~NPR 174.4 lakh excluding land
- preferred debt <=30–40%

## Phase status

### Phase 0 — Research & feasibility: PLANNING COMPLETE

### Phase 1 — Farm concept: COMPLETE
Herd concept, breeding strategy, feed acreage, farm functional design, equipment philosophy and manure flow defined.

### Phase 2 — Investment case: MODEL COMPLETE / EXTERNAL VALIDATION PENDING
CAPEX/OPEX, price sensitivity, debt policy, subsidy policy and go/no-go gates defined. Pending real quotations, milk-buyer term sheet and bank offer.

### Phase 3 — Build & procurement: PLAYBOOK COMPLETE / NOT EXECUTED
Tender, build sequence, commissioning and animal-procurement gates defined.

### Phase 4 — Launch & stabilization: PLAYBOOK COMPLETE / NOT EXECUTED
Intake, quarantine, 30/90/180-day stabilization routines and actual-vs-model governance defined.

### Phase 5 — Scale: ROADMAP COMPLETE / NOT EXECUTED
30 -> 50 -> 75–100 -> 150–200 only through performance gates. Revenue sequence defined.

## Core project rules

1. Feed before cows.
2. Cooling is production equipment.
3. Buy individual animals, not a seller's story.
4. Peak milk is not the breeding objective; lifetime profit is.
5. No subsidy in base-case viability.
6. No expansion while existing herd KPIs are red.
7. No automation without ROI.
8. No processing before raw-milk operations are stable.
9. Keep buyer, feed, reproduction and finance records from Day 1.
10. Every major decision goes into `docs/08-decisions/`.

## What is still genuinely unknown

A blueprint cannot replace physical and commercial facts. Before spending:
- exact site/land and irrigation capacity,
- milk buyer and price formula,
- cattle lot availability/quality,
- construction BOQ quotations,
- equipment quotations,
- actual Bardiya feed input prices,
- bank interest/collateral terms,
- current grant calls,
- water/electricity tests.

These are not planning gaps; they are **procurement/validation inputs**. They become DEC records when confirmed.

## Investment approval standard

Do not release major CAPEX until all Phase-2 GO gates in `PHASE_0_FEASIBILITY.md` and `INVESTMENT_CASE.md` are satisfied.
