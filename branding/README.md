## Usage notes

The stamp should be manufactured only after the registration certificate confirms the exact registered firm name and address. The stamp should not contain citizenship numbers, personal phone numbers, partner names, capital amount, or unnecessary partnership wording.

The logo is a branding asset and is separate from the government-facing stamp. The round stamp should remain simple enough to reproduce clearly at approximately 40 mm diameter.
