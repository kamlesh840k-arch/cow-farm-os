# Farm Vision

## Working vision

Develop a modern, profitable and scalable dairy farm in Bardiya, Nepal that wins through operational excellence rather than unnecessary capital intensity.

## Core principles

1. **Feed first** — herd size must be supported by reliable, economical feed and fodder.
2. **Genetics for our climate** — choose animals for lifetime profitability, fertility, heat tolerance and health, not litres alone.
3. **Breed our future herd** — build a strong replacement pipeline from selected females instead of depending permanently on purchased adult cows.
4. **Cow comfort is production infrastructure** — ventilation, shade, water, resting space, flooring and drainage are economic inputs.
5. **Measure reproduction** — calving interval, conception, services per conception, age at first calving and pregnancy rate must be tracked.
6. **Phase automation** — design for future mechanization, but buy machines only when labour savings, consistency or scale justify them.
7. **Separate evidence from decisions** — benchmark farms inspire us; they do not automatically define our farm.
8. **Design to expand** — avoid rebuilding core infrastructure every time the herd grows.

## Long-term possibilities

- Commercial milk production
- In-house replacement herd
- Advanced breeding/genetics program
- Fodder and silage production
- Manure compost and/or biogas
- Training/research partnerships
- Value-added dairy processing if later economics support it

No long-term expansion item is considered approved until supported by a formal decision record.
