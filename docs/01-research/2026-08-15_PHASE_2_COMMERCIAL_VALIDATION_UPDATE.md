# Phase 2 Commercial Validation Research Update — 2026-08-15

## Executive finding

The 40–60 total-animal launch remains a sensible **validation range**, but it is not yet financeable on current evidence. The integrated screening model shows Base DSCR of only 0.58x at 40% debt and Downside losses. Therefore the project remains NO-GO. The scale should be revised only after real buyer, feed, site, cattle, construction and finance terms are loaded; current data does not support expanding the launch hypothesis.

## Material research findings

### Milk routes

DDC's Nepalgunj Milk Supply Scheme identifies Bardiya in its work area, lists a 1,000 L Bhurigaun and 3,000 L Sanoshree chilling centre, and reports scheme collection of roughly 4–5 thousand L/day in lean season and 12–14 thousand L/day in flush season. This confirms a formal network near the project but not capacity reserved for Cow Farm OS, price, deductions or payment reliability.

The 2019 LI-BIRD Bardiya dairy value-chain study provides historical leads including Bhagawati Milk Producer's Cooperative in Badhaiya, DDC Kohalpur and Anupam Dairy. All must be revalidated.

### Site and climate

Badhaiyatal's municipal profile describes lowland elevation of 143–157 m and historical maximum temperature of 42.2°C. Cooling water, shade, airflow and backup power are therefore production infrastructure. Bardiya flood/waterlogging exposure makes surveyed levels, drainage outfall and monsoon access mandatory.

The national groundwater plan shows material Bardiya irrigation potential, but district potential cannot substitute for a selected-site bore yield/quality test.

### Finance

NRB's current first-amended concessional-loan procedure covers eligible agriculture/livestock and milk-production activities up to NPR 5 crore. It caps programme lender pricing at base +1.5%, provides a 3% interest subsidy for up to five years and requires relevant local branch recommendation above NPR 10 lakh, subject to bank appraisal and documentation.

ADBL's Ashadh-end 2083 base rate was 4.56%; its Shrawan 2083 general agriculture term premium is published up to 2.5%. If the loan qualifies for the current concessional framework, the tighter programme cap should govern. Only a sanction letter can confirm which product and rate apply.

### Insurance

The 2082 crop/livestock premium-support procedure remains current. Agriculture Diary 2083 describes support of 80% for insured value up to NPR 50 lakh, 65% from NPR 50 lakh to 1 crore and 50% above NPR 1 crore. The insurer must confirm valuation, tier application, farmer cash outlay, exclusions and claim procedure.

The Diary's planning valuation references—improved milch cow up to NPR 150,000 and improved heifer over one year up to NPR 75,000 or actual lower—may be below purchase allowances. This creates an uninsured-value/collateral risk that must be negotiated.

### FY2083/84 programmes

Lumbini's FY2083/84 budget includes cow/buffalo in an integrated intensive commercial value-chain development programme with a NPR 2.33 crore programme envelope and technical-service allocations. No current evidence establishes a farm-specific award. The Base and Downside models therefore include no grant.

### Cattle sourcing and veterinary support

NLBO currently publishes Holstein/Jersey pedigree and PPRS information, confirming breeding-network leads. It does not confirm live animal availability, price or records. Bardiya IALDO lists cattle/buffalo treatment and laboratory services; confirm which pre-purchase tests can be performed and turnaround time before agreeing deposits.

## Financial screening

The new formula-driven workbook includes scenarios, CAPEX/funding, monthly cash flow, 84-month debt schedule, gates, checks and sources. Formula checks pass.

Base assumptions: 50 total animals, 60% lactating, 13 L/day saleable yield per lactating animal, 97% saleable fraction, NPR 65/L, NPR 350/day lactating feed, 40% debt, 7.06% gross rate, seven-year tenor and 30-day buyer payment delay.

Results:

- screening funding envelope NPR 23.60m;
- Base annual saleable milk 138,080 L;
- Base EBITDA NPR 1.00m;
- Base cash cost NPR 59.7/L;
- Base annual debt service NPR 1.71m;
- Base DSCR 0.58x;
- Downside DSCR -1.49x;
- Upside DSCR 3.89x.

The model does not show that the project is impossible. It shows that current assumptions are too uncertain and the Base capital/debt structure is not self-financing. Likely levers are stronger buyer realization, lower ration cost, lower quote-backed CAPEX, more equity, phased cattle intake or better proven yield. These are hypotheses to negotiate and test, not targets to force.

## Immediate field actions

1. Take the buyer validation pack to DDC/Bardiya routes and at least two private/cooperative alternatives.
2. Shortlist two sites and commission survey, flood/drainage, water and irrigation evidence.
3. Obtain a nutritionist ration and three delivered feed/ingredient quotes.
4. Issue the cattle-source questionnaire through NLBO/PPRS/vet networks and collect individual records.
5. Tender the engineering basis to three comparable providers.
6. Submit one standardized evidence pack to ADBL and two other banks, plus two insurers.
7. Refresh the workbook and gate register; record a formal decision.
