# Banjara Agro Research Index

## Purpose

Study Banjara Agro as a benchmark for modern dairy farming in Nepal, while separating observed practices from assumptions and our own decisions.

## Research questions

1. What cattle breeds and bloodlines are actually used?
2. What is the herd composition: calves, heifers, dry cows, lactating cows and bulls?
3. What milk yield is achieved per cow and per lactation?
4. What is the calving interval and reproduction protocol?
5. What semen, AI and genetic-selection strategy is used?
6. How are calves and replacement heifers raised?
7. What is the shed system, stocking density, ventilation and cooling approach?
8. What is the feed program: silage, green fodder, dry matter, concentrate and TMR?
9. How much fodder land is needed relative to herd size?
10. What milking equipment and automation are used?
11. How are manure, slurry, compost and biogas handled?
12. What labour structure is used?
13. What is the reported investment and which parts are essential vs optional?
14. How does Banjara Agro earn revenue beyond raw milk, if at all?
15. Which systems are transferable to a capital-efficient Bardiya farm?

## Source handling

For every source capture:

- Title
- URL/source platform
- Publisher/channel
- Publication date if available
- Date reviewed
- Claims made
- What is directly visible vs verbally claimed
- Confidence level
- Conflicts with other sources
- Lesson for our farm
