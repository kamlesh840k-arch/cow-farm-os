# Banjara Agro Public Source Inventory

Reviewed: 2026-08-14

## Scope

This inventory records all relevant public sources discoverable in the current sweep. It includes first-party pages, major Nepal media reports, World Bank material, social mirrors, podcasts, trade/import records, and discoverable video references.

It does **not** claim visibility into private, deleted, unlisted, newly uploaded, or search-unindexed YouTube content.

## Major independent media sources

1. Capital Nepal — 2021
   - `विराटनगरको दरैयामा ५० करोड लगानीमा नेपालको पहिलो प्रविधियुक्त ‘बन्जरा एग्रो फार्म’`
   - https://www.capitalnepal.com/detail/19312
   - Early-stage farm: 27 cows, target 200, Holstein-Jersey description, sexed-semen plan, mechanization, biogas, greenhouse, milk/home delivery, financing.

2. Grameen Khabar — early expansion report
   - `विराटनगरका दुई युवक होटल व्यवसाय छोडेर कृषि उद्यममा आकर्षण`
   - https://grameenkhabar.com/8931
   - Reports 800 L/day at that stage, target 1,500 L/day and eventually 5,000 L/day; automated dairy intent.

3. Rastriya Samachar Samiti syndication / Arthik Awaj — 2022
   - `उत्कृष्ट फार्मको रुपमा विकसित हुँदै बन्जरा एग्रो`
   - https://arthikawaj.com/banjara-agro-farm/
   - 112 Holstein-Jersey cows; milk-yield claims; 1,000 L/day sales; silage centers; staff; grant; vegetables.

4. eNepalese — 2022 RSS syndication
   - `नमुना कृषि फार्म बन्जरा एग्रो फार्म`
   - https://www.enepalese.com/2022/04/351426.html
   - Similar 2022 dataset; useful as a duplicate/corroborating syndication, not an independent dataset.

5. Capital Nepal — 2022
   - `मामा–भान्जाकाे बन्जरा एग्राे फार्मः मासिक १५ लाख आम्दानी, ३० जनालाई रोजगारी`
   - https://www.capitalnepal.com/detail/25285
   - Retail/farm-to-home model; 112 cows; milk, curd and paneer sales; investment; staff.

6. Kantipur — 2023
   - `गजेन्द्रको कृषि फर्म : मेसिनबाटै काम, दिनमै बेच्छन् १ हजार लिटर दूध`
   - https://ekantipur.com/business/2023/07/05/168855615058398647.html
   - Strongest detailed operational snapshot found: 145 mother cows, ~1,100 L/day sales, 40 crore invested, automation, specialist vet, maize contract farming, 800 t silage, 42 workers.

7. Kantipur — 2025
   - `३२ संस्थालाई करोड–करोडका कृषि अनुदान : अधिकांशले गरे दुरुपयोग`
   - https://ekantipur.com/News-Folder/2025/02/02/32-the-organization-would-have-agricultural-grants-abuse-most-did-03-33.html
   - Banjara section reports ~180 cattle including calves, ~1,300 L/day, own breeding replacing imports, 50+ jobs, milking parlour, 3,000 L chiller, cow shower, silage/feed storage. Important: the article's headline concerns subsidy misuse broadly, but the Banjara section is presented as a `नमुना एग्रो` case; it does not itself state that Banjara misused its grant.

8. World Bank — 2025
   - `Laying Foundation for the Modern Livestock Sector in Nepal`
   - https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/feature/2025/02/19/laying-foundation-for-the-modern-livestock-sector-in-nepal
   - Identifies Banjara Agro Farm as one successful venture supported by NLSIP.
