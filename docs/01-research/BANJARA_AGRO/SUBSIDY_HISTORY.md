# Banjara Agro — Subsidy / Grant History

**Updated:** 2026-08-15  
**Entity:** Banjara Agro Pvt. Ltd., Biratnagar-12, Morang, Nepal

## Confirmed amount

### Nepal Livestock Sector Innovation Project (NLSIP)
- **Confirmed public grant amount:** **NPR 1,00,00,000 (NPR 1 crore)**.
- **Reported purpose:** Milking parlour / dairy modernization support.
- Contemporary 2022 reports from Rastriya Samachar Samiti-syndicated coverage, Arthik Awaj and Capital Nepal all state that the federal government, through the Livestock Innovation Project, provided Banjara Agro **NPR 1 crore** for the milking parlour.
- A 2025 Kantipur investigation lists Banjara Agro Pvt. Ltd. among institutions that received **more than NPR 1 crore** in agricultural grants, but the article does **not** state a precise Banjara-specific lifetime amount.
- The same Kantipur reporting says a **3,000-litre chiller** at Banjara was purchased with grant funds. Public reporting does not establish whether this was part of the same NPR 1 crore NLSIP package or a separate/additional grant.

## NLSIP financing structure context

The World Bank states that NLSIP used a matching-grant model generally structured as:
- **50% project matching grant**
- **30% bank/BFI financing**
- **20% beneficiary equity**

Therefore, if Banjara's NPR 1 crore represented the full 50% matching-grant share under the standard structure, the associated eligible subproject would have been approximately **NPR 2 crore**. This is an inference and should not be treated as a confirmed Banjara project cost unless the original grant agreement is obtained.
