# Banjara Agro — Subsidy / Grant History

**Updated:** 2026-08-15  
**Entity:** Banjara Agro Pvt. Ltd., Biratnagar-12, Darahiya, Morang, Nepal

## Executive finding

The strongest public evidence supports a **NPR 1,00,00,000 (NPR 1 crore)** federal matching grant to Banjara Agro under the **Nepal Livestock Sector Innovation Project (NLSIP)** for dairy mechanization / milking-parlour modernization. Later reporting confirms that a **3,000-litre milk chiller** was also purchased using grant money. Public sources reviewed to date do not prove a second, separately sanctioned Banjara grant, so the verified cumulative amount remains **at least NPR 1 crore**, with exact lifetime subsidy still unresolved.

## Confirmed grant

### Nepal Livestock Sector Innovation Project (NLSIP)
- **Confirmed public grant amount:** **NPR 1 crore**.
- **Reported purpose in 2022:** milking parlour / dairy modernization.
- **Implementing government agency:** Nepal Government, Ministry of Agriculture and Livestock Development through NLSIP.
- **External financing source:** World Bank financing to the Government of Nepal; this should not be described as a direct World Bank cash grant to Banjara.
- **Location eligibility:** Morang was one of the NLSIP implementation districts in Koshi Province.
- **Beneficiary type:** private agricultural/livestock enterprise.

Contemporary RSS-syndicated reporting dated April 2022 states that the federal government provided Banjara Agro **NPR 1 crore from the Livestock Innovation Project for a milking parlour**.

| Asset/component | Evidence | Classification |
|---|---|---|

## NLSIP matching-grant structure

Official/World Bank and project reporting describes the NLSIP business-plan matching-grant model as:
- **50% NLSIP matching grant**
- **30% bank/BFI loan financing**
- **20% beneficiary equity**

The programme allowed grants broadly ranging from **NPR 10 lakh to NPR 1 crore** under the relevant matching-grant framework. That means Banjara's publicly reported **NPR 1 crore** appears to sit at the **top end / maximum ceiling** of the programme as publicly described.

### Implied project economics

If Banjara's NPR 1 crore represented the complete 50% NLSIP share under the standard 50/30/20 structure, then the associated eligible subproject would imply approximately:

| Funding source | Standard share | Implied amount |
|---|---:|---:|
| NLSIP matching grant | 50% | NPR 1.00 crore |
| Bank/BFI financing | 30% | ~NPR 60 lakh |
| Banjara beneficiary equity | 20% | ~NPR 40 lakh |
| **Eligible subproject total** | **100%** | **~NPR 2.00 crore** |

**Important:** this is a structural inference from the standard programme model, not proof of Banjara's actual bank drawdown or equity contribution. The original grant agreement is required to verify those two amounts.

## Other conditions/features of the programme relevant to Banjara

- **Competitive selection:** NLSIP reporting says grants were not automatic; eligible enterprises were competitively selected because programme budget was limited.
- **Private-firm track:** NLSIP implemented a third-stage/track for private firms in addition to farmer groups/cooperatives.
- **Mandatory livestock insurance:** project reporting states livestock insurance was mandatory to receive grant/technical support.
- **Bank participation:** selected enterprises had to demonstrate bank financing for the 30% component under the standard model.
- **Beneficiary equity:** the enterprise was expected to contribute 20% itself.
- **Climate-smart / productivity orientation:** the project encouraged climate-smart technologies, higher-value equipment, productivity improvement, value addition and stronger dairy-market linkages.
- **Project period:** NLSIP operated from 2018 and concluded in 2024 after extensions/restructuring.

## Relationship to Banjara's other financing

A 2022 RSS report also stated that Banjara had around **NPR 9 crore of Mega Bank debt** related to farm infrastructure. This is **not a subsidy** and should not automatically be treated as the 30% NLSIP bank component. The public sources do not establish whether any portion of that larger bank borrowing was specifically pledged against the NLSIP subproject.

## Scale of Banjara investment versus grant

Public reports give different farm-investment snapshots at different dates:
- January 2022 OnlineKhabar reported about **NPR 8 crore already invested**, with a longer-term ambition to reach NPR 50 crore.
- April 2022 RSS reporting said **more than NPR 30 crore** had been spent and separately mentioned the NPR 1 crore NLSIP grant.
- 2025 Kantipur again described the farm as having more than **NPR 30 crore** invested.

Therefore the grant was important for automation but was only one component of a much larger privately/debt-financed farm investment.

## Later verification and status

- In 2023, NLSIP/Koshi reporting quoted Sanjay Chapagain of Banjara Agro describing the experience of applying for and obtaining grant money; Banjara was presented as an example of a sustainable modern farm.
- In February 2025, the World Bank identified **Banjara Agro Farm as one of the successful ventures supported by NLSIP**.
- A 2025 Kantipur investigation lists Banjara Agro among organizations that received **more than NPR 1 crore in agricultural grants**, but its Banjara section does not disclose a second grant amount or a precise cumulative figure.

## What is NOT yet proven

1. Whether Banjara received **more than one separate grant sanction**.
2. Whether the 3,000 L chiller was purchased under the same NPR 1 crore package or an additional grant.
3. Exact make/model and purchase price of the milking parlour.
4. Exact make/model and purchase price of the 3,000 L chiller.
5. Exact Banjara bank-loan amount tied to the NLSIP subproject.
6. Exact Banjara equity amount tied to the NLSIP subproject.
7. Whether cow shower, biogas, silage centre, feed equipment, cattle or other systems were paid by government grant.
8. Exact cumulative subsidy received by Banjara from federal, provincial and local programmes over its lifetime.
