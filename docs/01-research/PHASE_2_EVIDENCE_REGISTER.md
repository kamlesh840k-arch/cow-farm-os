## Overall project status — 2026-08-15

### Strategic concept: **PASS**
- 40–60 total animals at stabilized Phase 1.
- Infrastructure master-planned for ~100.
- 12–14 L/day underwriting yield.
- Feed security and buyer reliability are primary economics.
- Subsidy excluded from base-case viability.

### Commercial GO decision: **NO-GO / VALIDATION IN PROGRESS**

This is not a rejection of the project. It means critical farm-specific evidence is not yet available. The project should continue research, quotations, buyer engagement and site validation, but should not commit to cattle purchase or irreversible construction yet.

## Highest-priority next evidence

1. Select candidate site(s); obtain dimensions, flood/drainage, water and irrigation facts.
2. Obtain 3 written milk buyer/collection offers.
3. Obtain delivered feed quotations and develop nutritionist ration/cost per litre.
4. Identify traceable cattle suppliers and collect live quotations + records.
5. Get concept engineering + BOQs for 40–60 launch / ~100 master plan.
6. Obtain ADBL + 2 competing bank term sheets.
7. Obtain at least 2 livestock insurance quotations/policy wordings.
8. Recheck official FY2083/84 subsidy calls immediately before procurement.
9. Build the final monthly integrated financial model and downside stress test.

## GO conditions

The investment may advance to build only when:
- buyer capacity and price/payment terms are documented;
- feed/roughage/water plan is feasible and secure;
- traceable animals can be sourced at modeled cost/quality;
- quote-backed CAPEX and working capital are funded;
- downside cash flow services debt;
- insurance and biosecurity are operationally feasible;
- site/legal/environmental requirements are resolved;
- construction design complies with welfare and heat-stress requirements.
