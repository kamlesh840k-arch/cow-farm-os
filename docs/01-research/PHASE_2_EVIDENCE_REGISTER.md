## GO checklist

A GO decision requires all of the following:

- primary and backup buyer terms loaded into the model;
- selected site passes flood, water, access, drainage, power and tenure checks;
- annual ration/feed supply is feasible and fully costed;
- traceable animals are available at modeled quality/cost;
- quote-backed CAPEX, contingency and working capital are funded;
- Downside DSCR ≥1.20x and minimum cash stays positive;
- insurance is binding before movement;
- compliance and environmental pathway is cleared;
- engineering meets welfare/heat/milk-hygiene requirements;
- no grant or interest subsidy is necessary for Base/Downside viability.

Until then the permitted activity is research, testing, design, quotation and negotiation only.
