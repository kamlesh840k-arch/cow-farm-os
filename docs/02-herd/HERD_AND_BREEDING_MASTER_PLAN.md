# Herd & Breeding Master Plan

**Status:** Working genetic strategy approved for procurement planning; final animals depend on supplier availability and individual records.

## Foundation herd

Target: **30 breeding females**.

Recommended purchase mix:
- 20 adapted HF-cross females, primarily 2nd–3rd lactation, locally proven or proven in comparable Terai climate.
- 6 adapted Jersey-cross females.
- 4 in-calf heifers from the better-documented of the two lines.

The herd should not be built from pure exotic animals simply because their peak yield is higher.

## Why two genetic cohorts initially?

Bardiya-specific field data show a trade-off: Holstein crosses had higher peak milk but longer inter-calving interval than Jersey crosses. The first 24 months will therefore operate as a controlled commercial comparison.

Each animal receives an ID and is tagged by genetic cohort. Compare:
- saleable milk/day,
- feed cost/litre,
- conception rate,
- calving interval,
- mastitis,
- lameness,
- summer milk drop,
- culling,
- lifetime gross margin.

After 24 months, replacement genetics should follow whichever cohort produces better **lifetime profit**, not peak yield.

## Purchase specification

Every purchased cow/heifer should have:
- permanent ID/ear tag,
- age and parity,
- calving date,
- current pregnancy status and service date,
- 7-day observed milk record where lactating,
- udder and teat examination,
- feet/leg score,
- body condition score,
- mastitis screening,
- reproductive examination,
- vaccination/deworming history where available,
- independent veterinarian fitness certificate,
- transport and quarantine plan.

Reject animals with chronic mastitis, blind quarter, severe teat defects, major hoof/leg problems, persistent infertility, unexplained low body condition, fever/respiratory disease or unverifiable identity.

## Reproductive targets

Planning targets:
- age at first calving: 24–30 months depending on growth and genotype,
- first service after calving: normally 60–90 days only when clinically ready,
- calving interval target: <=13.5 months herd average by Year 3,
- services/conception target: <=1.8,
- pregnancy diagnosis: 30–45 days after AI, reconfirm later,
- dry period: ~50–70 days,
- annual cull/death allowance: 18% planning ceiling; investigate if exceeded.

## Semen strategy

1. Use sex-sorted semen on the best heifers and top cows when body condition, health and cyclicity are favorable.
2. Use conventional dairy semen on average animals where sexed-semen conception economics are weak.
3. Do not breed replacements from chronically infertile, mastitic, structurally weak or heat-intolerant cows.
4. Select sires for fertility, calving ease, udder, feet/legs, productive life and heat resilience—not milk PTA alone.
5. Maintain the two genetic lines deliberately; avoid random HF × Jersey crossing during the evaluation period unless a specific breeding decision is recorded.

## Female-calf policy

A female calf is not automatically a replacement.

Retain only calves meeting:
- healthy birth and colostrum record,
- strong dam performance,
- acceptable sire merit,
- target growth curve,
- no major congenital/structural defect,
- need within the approved replacement/expansion plan.

Once the adult herd reaches 50, surplus high-quality heifers become a planned sales stream.

## Calf protocol

- Clean calving pen.
- Dip navel promptly.
- Colostrum as early as possible; record time and amount.
- Separate clean calf housing with dry bedding and airflow.
- Fresh water and starter feed early.
- Weaning based on starter intake/growth rather than age alone.
- Monthly body weight or heart-girth tracking.

## Herd groups

Maintain separate groups for:
- fresh/high cows,
- mid/late lactation,
- dry/close-up cows,
- breeding-age heifers,
- younger heifers,
- pre-weaned calves,
- hospital/quarantine animals.

## 5-year adult-herd ceiling

Year 1: 27 average adults during ramp-up  
Year 2: 30  
Year 3: 36  
Year 4: 43  
Year 5: 50

No automatic expansion beyond 50. A second expansion decision requires a new feed, housing, buyer and cash-flow review.

## Core KPI dashboard

- lactating cows / adult cows
- saleable milk / lactating cow / day
- days open
- services/conception
- pregnancy rate
- calving interval
- abortion/stillbirth rate
- calf mortality 0–60 days
- heifer age/weight at first service
- mastitis incidence
- cull/death rate
- summer vs winter milk yield
- feed cost/litre by genetic cohort
