# Sexed Semen Supply Chain — Nepalgunj/Bardiya

**Research date:** 2026-08-21

## Confirmed government route
- National Livestock Breeding Office (NLBO), Nepalgunj is the government semen/LN2 hub supporting Bardiya.
- Bardiya recorded 5,021 cattle AI services in FY 2081/82, confirming an active field AI network.
- NLBO Nepalgunj produces/distributes conventional Jersey and Holstein semen and liquid nitrogen.
- Current Nepalgunj bull catalogue includes Jersey bulls NJ-04 RAJ, NJ-3 HARI, NJ-2 SAILA, NJ-1 PREM and Holstein bulls NHF-2 BENI, NHF-1 MALATI, NHF-4 KHIMA, NHF-3 NANDU.

## Sexed semen status
- Nepal has historically imported sexed HF and Jersey semen; government breeding strategy documents record about 9,000 sexed cattle semen doses imported historically, mainly Jersey and HF.
- Government procurement specifications for sexed Jersey semen have required ~90% female-calf accuracy and >60% first-service conception under ideal field conditions.
- I could not verify a public FY 2083/84 Nepalgunj stock list showing sexed HF/Jersey semen currently on hand. Therefore current government sexed-semen availability must be confirmed directly with the Semen/Nitrogen Distribution Section before planning breeding.

## Private route
- Private/imported sexed semen is known in the Nepal/India market from brands such as ABS, WWS, Semex, ST Genetics and others, but no current Nepalgunj/Bardiya authorized private distributor with published stock and price was independently verified in this research pass.
- Indicative India-market prices found online range widely; they should not be used as Nepal farm budget values without a Nepal supplier invoice and cold-chain verification.

## Procurement rule for Cow Farm OS
Before using any sexed straw, record:
- company/station
- bull name + code
- breed
- sexed technology/source
- batch/lot
- female-bias claim
- expiry/production details
- cold-chain custody
- AI technician
- straw cost + service cost

Do not buy anonymous "female semen" straws.

## Recommendation for 10-cow launch
- Use sexed semen first on the healthiest, best-fertility heifers/young cows.
- If no pregnancy after 1–2 properly timed sexed services, switch to good conventional semen rather than repeatedly losing cycles.
- Do not buy an LN2 tank for 10 cows; use the existing AI technician/network.
