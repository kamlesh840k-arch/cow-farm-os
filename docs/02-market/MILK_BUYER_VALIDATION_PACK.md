# Milk Buyer Validation Pack

Use this pack for DDC, cooperative and private buyers. Attach written evidence; do not mark a verbal answer as confirmed.

## Farm planning volume presented to buyer

- Launch planning range: 40–60 total animals.
- Current 50-head screening: about 296 L/day Downside, 378 L/day Base, 456 L/day Upside.
- Expansion: request indicative capacity for about 100 total animals, but do not promise volume.
- Milk: raw cow milk; composition and cooling specification to be agreed.

## Buyer questions

### Identity and capacity

1. Legal buyer/collection-centre name, address, registration and responsible contact.
2. Current lean/flush daily collection and remaining capacity.
3. Maximum guaranteed daily acceptance from this farm at launch.
4. Indicative capacity after expansion; any seasonal quota/cut.
5. Collection route, distance, pickup times, holidays and missed-pickup process.

### Price and quality

6. Current base milk standard and effective date.
7. Exact fat/SNF calculation with worked example.
8. Volume/season/quality bonuses; transport, chilling, testing or cooperative deductions.
9. Sampling/testing method, equipment calibration, dispute and retest process.
10. Temperature, microbial, adulteration, water, acidity and antibiotic-residue rules.
11. Rejected milk responsibility, notice and written test evidence.

### Payment and risk

12. Invoice/statement cycle, payment days, method, retention/security and current arrears.
13. Who bears transport loss and when title/risk transfers.
14. Termination/price-change notice and minimum contract period.
15. Three current supplier references, including at least one similar-volume farm.

### Infrastructure interface

16. Is on-farm chilling mandatory? Required capacity/temperature and maximum holding time.
17. Who supplies cans/tanker, weighs, cleans and seals samples?
18. Power/pickup failure procedure and backup route.

## Reference-check questions

- How long have you supplied the buyer and at what typical litres/day?
- How many days after delivery is cash actually received?
- Any unpaid balance or unexplained deduction in the last 12 months?
- Are fat/SNF results consistent with independent checks?
- Does the buyer accept all contracted milk in flush season and collect on holidays?
- What happened during the most recent pickup, quality or payment dispute?

## Gate decision

PASS only when a written primary offer covers full launch volume, a credible backup exists, actual realized price/payment assumptions are loaded into the model, and supplier references do not expose an unmitigated risk.
