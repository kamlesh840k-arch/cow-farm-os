# Phase 2 Engineering Basis and BOQ Request

**Status:** tender/validation basis, not construction drawings  
**Launch:** 40–60 total animals  
**Master plan:** about 100 total animals without demolition of core works

## Information the farm will provide

- cadastral/tenure and topographic survey;
- observed flood/waterlogging and drainage-outfall information;
- bore/source yield and water-quality test;
- grid/load/outage information;
- buyer pickup/chilling requirement;
- final herd groups and ration/feed inventory;
- applicable local approvals.

## Required design deliverables

1. Site master plan showing clean/dirty routes, vehicle/visitor controls, launch block and protected expansion lanes.
2. Group capacities: lactating, dry/close-up, maternity, sick, quarantine, heifer, calf and isolation.
3. Structural/civil drawings, levels, sections, roof/opening strategy, non-slip floors, feed alley, troughs and drains.
4. Heat-load basis with shade, orientation, ridge/open sides, fans/soakers, cooling-water and backup assumptions.
5. Water balance, source/pump/storage/trough sizing and at least one-day operational reserve.
6. Milk room/milking/chilling/pickup interface, hot water, cleaning/CIP and critical power.
7. Stormwater separation, manure/wastewater mass balance, compost/biogas interface and dead-stock route.
8. Electrical load schedule, earthing, protection and critical backup.
9. Fire, worker safety, animal welfare and biosecurity provisions.
10. Compliance matrix against the current Lumbini commercial cow/buffalo welfare standard and applicable livestock good-practice/milk-hygiene directives.
11. Construction phasing, commissioning tests, as-builts, manuals, warranty and training.

## Comparable quotation format

Each line: WBS, description/specification, unit, quantity, material rate, labour rate, plant/transport, amount, tax, lead time, exclusions, warranty and acceptance test. Separate optional expansion items from launch-critical items.

## Commissioning gates before cattle arrive

- water source, storage, distribution and trough flow tested;
- drainage/stormwater/manure routes wet-tested;
- fan/soaker/cooling and backup power tested at design load;
- milking cleaning, hot water and chilling/pickup interface accepted;
- quarantine/sick/calving separation complete;
- surfaces safe, cleanable and free of construction hazards;
- fire, fencing, access and biosecurity controls operational;
- feed storage and 90-day intake inventory ready;
- defects, manuals, spares and responsible operators recorded.
