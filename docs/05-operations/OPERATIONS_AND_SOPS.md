# Operations & SOP System

## Staffing — Phase 1

- 1 owner-manager or experienced farm manager
- 4 full-time worker equivalents
- contracted/retained veterinarian and AI technician
- seasonal fodder/harvest machinery support

Roles must be assigned by shift. No critical task should depend on one person only.

## Daily operating rhythm

### Early shift
1. Walk-through: sick/down/calving/heat-stressed animals.
2. Check water and cooling.
3. Pre-milking observation and udder check.
4. Milk using standardized prep, attachment and post-dip routine.
5. Record yield and withheld milk.
6. Feed fresh ration and push-up feed.
7. Clean milking equipment.
8. Scrape/collect manure without contaminating feed/milk route.
9. Calf feeding and health check.

### Midday
- heat-stress control,
- water-flow inspection,
- feed push-up/refusal check,
- treatments/AI/vet work,
- fodder/silage inventory work,
- repairs and bedding.

### Evening
Repeat milking, feeding, calf care, health check and equipment cleaning. Confirm chiller temperature and milk dispatch record.

## Milking SOP controls

- clean hands/gloves,
- forestrip and check abnormal milk,
- pre-dip/contact time/wipe dry,
- attach promptly,
- avoid overmilking,
- post-dip,
- segregate treated/withheld cows visibly,
- wash/CIP equipment after every milking,
- log mastitis and treatment.

## Milk-quality records

Daily:
- cow milk yield,
- bulk volume,
- buyer accepted volume,
- rejected/withheld volume,
- chiller temperature,
- fat/SNF if tested,
- antibiotic-withdrawal status.

Monthly:
- mastitis cases,
- bacterial/SCC information where available,
- buyer deductions,
- cleaning-chemical consumption.

## Health and biosecurity

- quarantine every purchased animal,
- visitor log and restricted animal areas,
- dedicated boots/tools where practical,
- vaccination plan signed by local veterinarian,
- parasite program based on local risk and diagnosis,
- immediate isolation of fever, respiratory, severe diarrhea or suspected contagious disease,
- necropsy/testing route for unexplained deaths,
- never use antibiotics without withdrawal records.

## Reproduction workflow

Daily heat observation -> AI record -> 30–45 day pregnancy check -> reconfirmation -> dry-off date -> close-up group -> calving record -> postpartum examination -> breeding eligibility.

## Calf SOP

- clean calving environment,
- navel disinfection,
- early colostrum with time/amount recorded,
- ID within 24 hours,
- daily milk/starter/water and health log,
- weekly/monthly growth tracking,
- rapid response to diarrhea/pneumonia.

## Feed SOP

- weigh or calibrate ingredients,
- maintain group ration sheet,
- measure refusals,
- inspect silage face every day,
- discard visibly spoiled/moldy feed,
- record bunker opening and estimated inventory,
- no abrupt ration change.

## Heat-stress SOP

When heat load rises:
- fans continuously where required,
- timed cow soaking at feed line/holding area,
- maximize clean water access,
- feed larger share in cooler hours,
- reduce holding time,
- record milk drop and respiratory/panting signs.

## Manure SOP

Fresh manure -> collection -> digester/compost route. Prevent clean stormwater from entering manure storage. Stabilize digestate before vermicomposting.

## Record system

Minimum animal fields:
ID, genetics, DOB/age, sire/dam where known, purchase cost, parity, calving dates, daily/weekly milk, AI dates, pregnancy results, disease, treatment, withdrawal, body condition, cull reason and sale value.

## Monthly management meeting

Review:
- litres/day,
- milk/cow,
- price/litre,
- feed cost/litre,
- total cash cost/litre,
- pregnancy and calving metrics,
- mastitis,
- calf mortality,
- feed inventory days,
- labour hours,
- receivables,
- variance from budget.

Any KPI outside threshold receives an owner, corrective action and due date.
