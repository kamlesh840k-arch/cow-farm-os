# FY2083/84 Finance, Insurance and Programme Update

**Updated:** 2026-08-15  
**Model treatment:** no grant or interest subsidy in Base/Downside viability.

## Concessional agriculture/livestock credit

NRB's first-amended 2082 procedure, published 2026-08-13, provides the current framework used for screening:

- eligible agriculture/livestock lending including livestock farming and milk production;
- facility ceiling up to NPR 5 crore;
- lender pricing cap under the programme of base rate +1.5%;
- government interest support of 3% for up to five years;
- bank appraisal, business registration/PAN/tax and proposal documentation;
- relevant local branch recommendation for loans above NPR 10 lakh.

The farm is not entitled merely because the activity is eligible. Bank sanction, collateral, equity, documentation and programme availability control.

ADBL reference points: Ashadh-end 2083 base rate 4.56%; published Shrawan 2083 general agriculture term premium up to 2.5%. For an eligible concessional facility, confirm the applicable tighter cap in the sanction letter.

## Livestock insurance

Current 2082 procedure and Agriculture Diary 2083 indicate tiered premium support:

- 80% for insured value up to NPR 50 lakh;
- 65% for insured value from NPR 50 lakh to NPR 1 crore;
- 50% above NPR 1 crore.

Planning valuation references in the Diary include improved milch cow up to NPR 150,000 and improved heifer over one year up to NPR 75,000 or actual lower. Because purchase allowances may exceed insured valuation, quantify the uninsured gap.

Obtain at least two written quotations covering animal identification, valuation, premium, supported portion, farmer payment, covered causes, exclusions, waiting period, health/vet evidence, reporting/claim deadline, salvage/carcass evidence, payout timing, deductible and finance assignment.

No animal moves without confirmed binding cover.

## FY2083/84 public programmes

Lumbini's budget includes cow/buffalo within integrated intensive commercial value-chain development, with a NPR 2.33 crore programme envelope. It also budgets technical services. This is not proof of a current farm-level call, eligibility or award.

Maintain a weekly watch:

- Badhaiyatal Rural Municipality;
- Integrated Agriculture & Livestock Development Office, Bardiya;
- Lumbini Directorate of Livestock and Fisheries Development;
- Lumbini agriculture ministry/economic affairs budget/notice pages;
- federal MoALD/DLS/NDDB notices.

For each open notice record publication/closing date, geography, applicant/entity rules, minimum scale, eligible assets, matching ratio, maximum support, tax/PAN/audit requirements, prior-purchase prohibition, duplicate-support rule, inspection, payment method and post-award obligations.

## Modeling rules

- Grants: zero in Base/Downside; receivable only after written award and eligible cost.
- Interest support: zero in Base/Downside; test expiry/refusal in stress case.
- Insurance support: use only after insurer confirms eligibility and cash flow; maintain gross-premium liquidity if reimbursement is delayed.
- Use lender gross rate for covenant and post-support stress.
- Include collateral valuation, fees, working capital and receivable delay—not CAPEX alone.
