## Leverage policy

Illustrative 7-year loan at 10% interest on the NPR 174.4 lakh total funding requirement:

| Debt share | Loan | Approx annual debt service |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 30% | NPR 52.32 lakh | NPR 10.42 lakh |
| 40% | NPR 69.76 lakh | NPR 13.90 lakh |
| 50% | NPR 87.20 lakh | NPR 17.37 lakh |
| 60% | NPR 104.64 lakh | NPR 20.85 lakh |

Therefore:
- preferred: **30–40% debt maximum** under the current model,
- 50%+ debt requires materially better validated milk price, yield or CAPEX,
- grant/subsidy must reduce equity requirement or accelerate payback; it is not counted in base revenue.

Actual interest rate, grace period and collateral terms must replace this scenario before financing.

## Downside tests

### Price shock
At NPR 65/L the operating surplus is thin. High leverage would make the project fragile.

### Milk-yield shock
If saleable milk/lactating cow falls 15% without equivalent feed-cost reduction, cost/litre rises sharply. Scaling is frozen until recovery.

### Feed inflation
Concentrate and straw price increases must be tested monthly. Own silage/green fodder is the main hedge.

### Heat/fertility shock
Longer days open reduce lactating fraction and replacement flow; cooling and breeding KPIs are financial controls, not only husbandry controls.

### CAPEX overrun
If quoted project cost exceeds NPR 174.4 lakh by >10%, return to investment committee/decision log before committing.
