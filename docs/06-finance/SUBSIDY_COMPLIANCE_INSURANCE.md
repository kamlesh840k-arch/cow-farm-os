# Subsidy, Compliance & Insurance Register

**Review date:** 2026-08-15 / FY 2083/84

## Policy

The farm must be financially viable **without subsidy**. Any grant, premium support, concessional credit or machinery support is upside and should reduce risk/capital burden rather than justify an otherwise weak project.

- business/farm registration certificate
- PAN/VAT documents as applicable
- proprietor/company/cooperative documents
- land ownership or lease agreement
- farmer registration where applicable
- citizenship/authorized-person documents
- bank details
- tax clearance where required
- project report/DPR
- farm layout and BOQ
- supplier quotations
- livestock inventory and IDs
- veterinary/health records
- photographs and geo/location evidence if required
- local recommendation letters if required
- self-declaration on non-duplication of subsidy
- insurance documents
- previous-grant disclosure

The Bardiya office publishes application/action-plan and non-double-subsidy declaration formats; use the current forms for each call.

## Insurance

Before cattle arrive:
- obtain livestock insurance quotations,
- confirm insured value methodology,
- identify death/disease exclusions,
- understand ear-tag/ID requirements,
- clarify claim notification timeline,
- verify the **current applicable government premium-subsidy percentage** with the insurer and active guideline/notice rather than using an old percentage from memory.

Government source: DLS lists the Crop and Livestock Insurance Premium Subsidy Guideline, 2081.

## Dairy hygiene and processing compliance

Raw-milk farm operations should follow the milk/milk-products hygiene and quality directive and livestock good-practice requirements.

If the business later processes/brands curd, paneer, ghee or packaged milk, complete the required DFTQC registration/licensing/quality steps **before** commercial sale.

Relevant sources:
- DFTQC directives: https://dftqc.gov.np/acts-rules/5/86576577
- DFTQC mandatory food/feed standards: https://dftqc.gov.np/acts-rules/12/99118576
- DLS procedures: https://dls.gov.np/acts-rules/4/57786467

## Governance rule

No subsidy is recorded as receivable until:
1. official call is published,
2. our farm is eligible,
3. application is submitted,
4. written approval/agreement exists.

No grant-dependent CAPEX order is placed before written approval.
