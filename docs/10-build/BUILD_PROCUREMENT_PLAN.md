# Phase 3 — Build & Procurement Plan

**Status:** Execution playbook complete; physical build not started by this document.

## Gate 3A — Land/site due diligence

Before final drawing:
- confirm legal access/tenure,
- map plot boundaries,
- topographic levels and drainage outlet,
- flood/high-water history,
- year-round road access,
- reliable water source and yield,
- electrical supply and backup feasibility,
- fodder-land irrigation,
- distance to milk buyer/collection route,
- manure/effluent setback and neighbor considerations.

## Gate 3B — Design package

Produce:
- scaled master plan,
- shed structural drawing,
- feed alley and cow-flow plan,
- calf/heifer/quarantine/calving plan,
- milk room/chiller plan,
- water-storage/piping layout,
- electrical load schedule,
- fan/soaker layout,
- stormwater vs manure drainage plan,
- silage bunker drawing,
- manure/biogas/compost flow,
- BOQ.

## Tender process

For major packages obtain at least 3 comparable quotations with the same specification.

Bid table fields:
- supplier,
- technical compliance,
- price excluding/including tax,
- delivery,
- warranty,
- installation,
- spares,
- service response,
- payment schedule,
- exclusions.

Select on lifecycle value, not lowest price alone.

## Construction sequence

1. Site grading, roads and drainage outlet.
2. Water source/storage and underground mains.
3. Main shed foundation/structure/roof.
4. Feed alley, floors and drains.
5. Calf/heifer/calving/quarantine structures.
6. Milk room and equipment utilities.
7. Silage bunkers and feed storage.
8. Manure collection/biogas/compost area.
9. Electrical system and backup.
10. Fans/soakers/water troughs.
11. Milking and chilling equipment.
12. Fencing/biosecurity/vehicle controls.
13. Commission and test every system before cows arrive.

## Animal procurement sequence

Never buy cows first and then rush construction.

Animals are selected only after:
- feed stock is ready,
- quarantine works,
- water and cooling are commissioned,
- milking/chilling are tested,
- worker training is complete,
- buyer is confirmed,
- insurance is active or binding terms are in place.

Purchase in batches if possible rather than moving 30 unfamiliar animals on one day. Maintain supplier and individual-cow records.

## Equipment — essential Phase 1

- reliable milking machine/small parlour,
- chiller sized to pickup interval,
- fodder chopper,
- weighing/calibration tools,
- fans and soakers,
- high-flow water system,
- basic restraint/treatment facility,
- milk testing/thermometer tools,
- calf feeding equipment,
- generator/backup for critical loads,
- manure scraping/handling tools.

## Equipment deferred until ROI gate

- large TMR mixer,
- automated feed pusher,
- large parlour,
- separator/processing line,
- automated manure scraper if manual/semi-mechanical system remains economical,
- advanced herd sensors unless they solve a measured problem.

## Procurement acceptance tests

Before final payment:
- milking vacuum/pulsation/cleaning test,
- chiller reaches required temperature under realistic volume,
- backup power starts critical loads,
- trough flow-rate test,
- fan/soaker coverage test,
- drains flow without pooling,
- bunker access and compaction path works,
- quarantine truly separates animals,
- all warranties/manuals/spares received.
