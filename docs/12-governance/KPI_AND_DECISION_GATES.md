# KPI Scorecard & Decision Gates

## North-star economics

1. Cash cost per saleable litre.
2. Saleable litres per adult breeding female per year.
3. Operating cash surplus after feed, labour, vet, utilities and maintenance.
4. Cash surplus after debt service.
5. Lifetime margin by genetic cohort.

## Weekly dashboard

- adult cows
- lactating cows
- saleable litres/day
- L/lactating cow/day
- milk rejected/withheld
- feed kg/day by ingredient
- feed cost/L
- silage inventory days
- sick cows
- mastitis cases
- calves with diarrhea/respiratory signs
- overdue receivable

## Monthly dashboard and thresholds

| KPI | Green | Warning | Stop/Correct |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Lactating fraction | >=75% | 68–74% | <68% |
| Saleable L/lactating cow/day | >=17 | 15–16.9 | <15 |
| Cash cost/L | <=60 | 60–65 | >65 |
| Feed inventory | >=90 days | 60–89 | <60 |
| Calf mortality 0–60d | <=5% rolling | 5–8% | >8% |
| Adult annualized cull/death | <=18% | 18–22% | >22% |
| Services/conception | <=1.8 | 1.8–2.2 | >2.2 |
| Calving interval | <=13.5 mo | 13.5–15 | >15 |
| Buyer payment delay | within contract | 1 cycle late | repeated/serious |

Thresholds are starting management limits and should be revised after local baseline data.

## Expansion gate — 30 to 50

Require six consecutive months with:
- green or approved performance on core KPIs,
- positive cash flow after scheduled debt service,
- feed land/inventory already increased,
- youngstock housing capacity,
- confirmed buyer capacity,
- capital available without breaching leverage policy.

## Machinery gate

Any machine above NPR 5 lakh requires a one-page ROI record:
- current bottleneck,
- baseline labour/cost/loss,
- purchase + installation,
- maintenance,
- expected annual cash benefit,
- payback,
- downside case,
- alternative manual/semi-mechanized option.

## Processing gate

No branded dairy processing until:
- raw milk business stable for at least 12 months,
- sufficient surplus milk,
- DFTQC path confirmed,
- cold chain and distribution proven,
- pilot gross margin beats selling raw milk,
- return/spoilage risk quantified.

## Decision log

Major choices require DEC records:
- final site,
- breed/genetics after 24-month comparison,
- milk buyer,
- financing,
- contractor,
- expansion to 50,
- expansion beyond 50,
- TMR/parlour automation,
- processing/value-added dairy.
