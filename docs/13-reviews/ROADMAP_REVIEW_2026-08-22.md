# Cow Farm OS — Roadmap Review — 2026-08-22

## Executive decision

A meaningful roadmap change is required: Phase 1 is now a **10-cow commercial pilot**, not a 40–60 animal launch. The operating plan should be optimized around proving buyer-payment reliability, feed economics, animal quality and reproduction before scaling 10 -> 20 -> 40 -> 60 -> ~100.

## Meaningful external changes / current signals

### 1. Milk-market payment risk remains material

Kantipur reported on 2026-08-18 that DDC still owed approximately **NPR 350 million** to farmers from 235 dairy cooperatives. The Agriculture Minister stated that DDC's payment delay had improved from about eight months to roughly **2–3 months**, but arrears remain.

**Roadmap consequence:** buyer payment-days must be a hard underwriting variable. The 10-cow financial model should include a 2–3 month receivable-delay stress case and avoid relying on a buyer solely because its headline milk price is higher.

### 2. FY2083/84 local/provincial programme cycle is opening, but no Bardiya dairy-specific call is yet verified

Badhaiyatal has published its FY2083/84 policy/economic documents. Bardiya IALDO's current public page still shows previous-year programme notices/forms and no newly verified FY2083/84 dairy-specific proposal call as of this review. Other Lumbini agriculture offices are beginning to publish FY2083/84 approved programmes, showing that the new-year programme cycle is active.

**Roadmap consequence:** contact Badhaiyatal livestock branch and Bardiya IALDO before buying any potentially subsidizable equipment. Do not delay a viable farm indefinitely for subsidy, but avoid purchasing assets immediately before a matching-grant notice if early purchase would make them ineligible.

### 3. Credit route remains relevant but must be bank-verified

Current NRB material continues to recognize **commercial agriculture and livestock loans** within the concessional-credit framework, with programme limits that can be far above the requirements of a 10-cow pilot. Public rules are not equivalent to an executable bank offer.

**Roadmap consequence:** obtain at least two written bank term sheets showing actual interest, subsidy treatment, collateral, grace period and repayment schedule. Exclude interest subsidy from the base-case until the bank confirms it.

### 4. Insurance process is increasingly digitized

Nepal Insurance Authority reports that agriculture/livestock policies are being issued through the **Agriculture and Livestock Insurance System (ALIS)**. Government premium support remains an important policy feature, but the exact amount applicable to the farm's animals must be confirmed at issuance.

**Roadmap consequence:** get quotations for the exact candidate cows before purchase; require written premium and government-share treatment rather than using a historical generic percentage in the model.

## Internal research gap that now matters most

The biggest gap is no longer national dairy research. It is **local executable evidence for a 10-cow Bardiya pilot**:

1. Three real buyer offers with fat/SNF formula, pickup timing and recent payment evidence.
2. Exact site + irrigated fodder area + water test.
3. At least 20 candidate cows screened to choose the best 10.
4. Current Bardiya/Nepalgunj cow prices and delivered feed prices.
5. Two insurer quotes and two bank term sheets.
6. Modular 10-cow civil/equipment BOQ.
7. Live confirmation of sexed-semen availability, bull code and cost through Nepalgunj/Bardiya AI channels.

## Updated decision order

1. Buyer/payment validation.
2. Site/fodder validation.
3. Cow procurement market scan and test protocol.
4. Insurance and bank terms.
5. FY2083/84 subsidy calendar check.
6. Engineering BOQ.
7. Downside financial model.
8. GO/NO-GO for first 10 cows.

## Current status

**NO-GO on immediate cow purchase today.** This is not because the project concept is weak; it is because the remaining questions are now highly local and executable. Once the local buyer/feed/cow/finance/insurance gates are documented, the 10-cow approach materially reduces capital-at-risk and is the preferred launch path.
